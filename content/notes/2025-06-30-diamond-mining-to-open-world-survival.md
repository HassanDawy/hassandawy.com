Our Algoverse team took a Minecraft agent trained to mine diamonds and
fine-tuned it toward open-world survival and exploration. The post covers how
reward design, curriculum training and methods for preventing forgetting shape
the agent's behavior, and the misaligned behaviors we saw along the way, like
tunneling underground instead of exploring.
