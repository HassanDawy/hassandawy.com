## The problem

How do you keep an agent aligned when it moves from a narrow goal (mine a
diamond) to open-world survival, where many more behaviors can earn reward?

## What I built

- Reward-based training systems for survival agents, and learning setups for
  progression tasks.
- RL fine-tuning pipelines in Python on MineRL, using RunPod for GPU-accelerated
  experiments.

## Results

I co-authored a technical research post on the work, published on LessWrong:
*From Diamond Mining to Open-World Survival: Alignment and Emergent Behavior in Minecraft Agents*.
